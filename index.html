WELCOME TO LOCAL VOICE

Local Voice is a dedicated platform where individuals can report local environmental issues directly to Enviro by AK, either publicly or privately. Our primary objective is to gather crowd-sourced data regarding destructive activities harming local ecosystems. Using this information, our company deploys planned, strategic actions to effectively address and resolve these environmental threats.
